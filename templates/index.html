How it works

Upload structured data (CSV, Excel, TXT, JSON). The app profiles columns, detects anomalies, renders plots and can produce AI summaries plus Q&A if a free‑tier Gemini model is available. Large time series are downsampled for speed.

You can switch views after processing: overview, static plots, interactive exploration, forecast or correlation maps.

If AI quota is exhausted you still get an offline heuristic summary with a visible reason tag so you know what happened.

Experimental data insight tool – adaptive to free-tier constraints. Refresh or re‑analyze to attempt a new AI summary if capacity frees up.
